How to Play ⌄
  1. Jump a munchie.

    Move one treat over another into an empty plate, horizontally or vertically.

  2. Serve it to Miso.

    The treat you jump over flies down to Miso and counts toward today's café order.

  3. Complete the order.

    Serve every treat shown above. Other treats may remain on the board.

Wrapped munchies need two jumps: the first opens the wrapper and the second serves the treat. Ceramic saucers are fixed supports: you can jump over them, but they never enter the order.

You can also tap a treat, then tap the empty plate. You start with 5 lives. If the order becomes impossible, you lose one life and return to the last proven safe position. One life recharges every 30 minutes.

You start with 5 hints and earn one the first time you finish each chapter. You can get another hint or life by watching the 3-second demo video.
